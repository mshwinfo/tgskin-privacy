謎之茶室 mizusalon 隱私權政策

Privacy Policy · 最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-25 · 開發者 / Developer: mshwinfo

謎之茶室 mizusalon 是為 HarmonyOS NEXT 開發的第三方 Telegram 客戶端，並非 Telegram 官方應用，與 Telegram 無隸屬關係，亦未獲其背書。本應用使用 Telegram 官方開放的 TDLib 函式庫與公開 API 與 Telegram 伺服器連線。
mizusalon is a third-party Telegram client for HarmonyOS NEXT. It is not an official Telegram app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram. The App connects to Telegram servers using Telegram's official open-source TDLib library and public API.

1. 我們不收集個人資料We do not collect personal data

開發者不收集、不接收、不保存任何使用者資料。本應用沒有任何開發者自有的伺服器。

The developer does not collect, receive or store any user data. The App has no developer-operated servers.

  • 帳號與訊息：您的手機號碼、驗證碼、密碼、聯絡人、對話與訊息內容，只在您的裝置與 Telegram 伺服器之間傳輸，依 Telegram 的隱私權政策處理。開發者無法存取。
    Account and messages: your phone number, verification codes, password, contacts, chats and message contents travel only between your device and Telegram's servers and are governed by Telegram's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to them.
  • 本地資料：登入狀態、訊息快取、對話列表與下載的媒體檔案儲存在本應用的裝置沙箱目錄，受系統保護，解除安裝即全部刪除。
    Local data: sign-in state, message cache, chat list and downloaded media are stored in the App's protected sandbox on your device and are deleted when the App is uninstalled.
  • 通話紀錄檔：語音通話時，通話元件會在本應用沙箱寫入一份連線除錯紀錄（只含連線狀態與網路資訊，不含聲音內容），每通覆寫、不會自動傳送給任何人，解除安裝即刪除。
    Call log file: during a voice call, the calling component writes a connection debug log to the App sandbox (connection state and network information only, no audio). It is overwritten on each call, never sent anywhere automatically, and deleted when the App is uninstalled.
  • 裝置資訊：TDLib 連線時會向 Telegram 回報裝置型號與系統版本（作為 Telegram「已登入裝置」清單的顯示用途），這是 Telegram 協定的必要欄位，開發者不另行收集。
    Device information: when connecting, TDLib reports the device model and OS version to Telegram so the session appears in your "Active Sessions" list. This is a required field of the Telegram protocol; the developer does not collect it separately.

2. 第三方服務Third-party services

  • Telegram：訊息服務提供者。The messaging service provider.
  • 翻譯服務：只有在您點選某則訊息的「翻譯」，或您自行在設定中開啟「自動翻譯」時，訊息文字才會送到您選擇的翻譯提供者（預設為「免費自動」：先用 Telegram 官方翻譯，失敗時改用 Google 翻譯；可改為只用其中一個）。自動翻譯預設關閉，只翻譯您正開著的對話中新收到的訊息。
    Translation: message text is sent to the translation provider you selected (by default "Free auto": Telegram's own translation first, falling back to Google Translate; you can pick just one) only when you tap "Translate" on a message, or when you turn on "Auto-translate" in Settings yourself. Auto-translate is off by default and only covers new incoming messages in the chat you have open.
  • AppGallery 更新檢查：本應用會透過華為 AppGallery 的更新服務查詢是否有新版本（啟動後一天最多一次，可在設定中關閉），由華為依其隱私政策處理。
    AppGallery update check: the App asks Huawei AppGallery's update service whether a newer version exists (at most once a day after launch; can be turned off in Settings). Huawei processes this under its own privacy policy.
  • 本應用不含廣告 SDK、分析 SDK 或任何追蹤元件。The App contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or tracking component.

MICROPHONE只在兩種情況使用：（1）您按下麥克風按鈕錄製語音訊息，錄音存在本應用沙箱，只傳送給您選擇的對話；（2）語音通話進行中，聲音經端對端加密後直接傳給通話對象（經由 Telegram 的中繼伺服器或點對點連線），本應用不錄製也不保存通話內容。第一次使用時由系統詢問，可隨時在系統設定關閉。
Used only in two cases: (1) while you record a voice message after tapping the microphone button — the recording stays in the App sandbox and is sent only to the chat you choose; (2) during a voice call — audio is end-to-end encrypted and sent directly to the other party (through Telegram relay servers or a peer-to-peer connection); the App does not record or store call audio. Requested by the system on first use and can be turned off in system settings at any time.

本應用不申請位置、相機、通訊錄或儲存讀取權限。拍照與錄影透過系統相機（Camera Picker）進行，本應用只取得您拍下並確認的那一張照片或那一段影片；選取相簿照片透過系統圖片選擇器進行，只取得您當下選定的項目。

The App does not request location, camera, contacts or storage read permissions. Taking photos and videos uses the system camera (Camera Picker), and the App only receives the photo or video you take and confirm. Choosing from your gallery uses the system picker and exposes only the items you select.

4. 兒童Children

本應用不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。Telegram 服務條款要求使用者年滿 16 歲（部分地區 13 歲）。

The App is not directed at children under 13. Telegram's Terms of Service require users to be at least 16 (13 in some regions).

5. 資料刪除Data deletion

在本應用內「登出」會請 Telegram 伺服器終止本裝置的登入階段並清除本地快取；解除安裝本應用會刪除沙箱內全部資料。您的 Telegram 帳號資料由 Telegram 保管，刪除帳號請依 Telegram 官方流程。

"Log out" inside the App asks Telegram to terminate this device's session and clears the local cache. Uninstalling the App removes all sandbox data. Your Telegram account data is held by Telegram; to delete your account, follow Telegram's official procedure.

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and a notice posted on the store listing.
