tgskin 隱私權政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-06-16

tgskin 並非 Telegram 官方應用，與 Telegram LLC 無關，僅以系統 WebView 載入公開的 Telegram Web 介面，不修改 Telegram 協議或伺服器行為。
tgskin is not an official Telegram app and is not affiliated with Telegram LLC. It only loads the public Telegram Web interface in a system WebView and does not modify Telegram's protocol or server behavior.

1. 我們不收集個人資料We do not collect personal data

tgskin 本身不收集、不儲存、不上傳任何個人資料或聊天內容。本應用沒有自己的伺服器或後端，也未內建分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。

tgskin itself does not collect, store, or upload any personal data or chat content. The app has no server or backend of its own and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. 你的 Telegram 資料Your Telegram data

你的 Telegram 帳號、訊息、聯絡人等資料，皆由 Telegram 官方 Web 服務（web.telegram.org）直接處理，受 Telegram 自身的隱私政策約束。tgskin 僅載入該網頁，不攔截、不讀取、不轉存其內容。

Your Telegram account, messages, and contacts are handled directly by the official Telegram Web service (web.telegram.org) and are governed by Telegram's own Privacy Policy. tgskin merely loads that page; it does not intercept, read, or relay its contents.

3. 本機儲存Local storage

為維持 Telegram Web 的登入狀態，系統 WebView 會在你的裝置本機儲存 Telegram Web 所需的 Cookie 與 localStorage，與一般瀏覽器行為相同。這些資料僅存於你的裝置，不會傳送給 tgskin 開發者或任何第三方。你可透過系統設定清除應用資料以移除它們。

To keep your Telegram Web session signed in, the system WebView stores the cookies and localStorage required by Telegram Web on your device, just like an ordinary browser. This data stays on your device and is never sent to the tgskin developer or any third party. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in system settings.

4. 權限Permissions

首發版僅申請以下權限：

  • INTERNET（網路）：載入 Telegram Web 與收發網頁訊息。
  • GET_NETWORK_INFO（網路狀態）：偵測連線狀態以顯示載入或異常提示。

本應用不申請相機、麥克風、定位、通訊錄或讀寫媒體等敏感權限。

The initial release requests only INTERNET (to load Telegram Web) and GET_NETWORK_INFO (to detect connectivity). It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or media storage permissions.

5. 檔案下載File downloads

當你主動下載檔案時，檔案會透過系統檔案選擇器儲存到你選擇的位置。下載行為由你發起，檔案不會被上傳至任何伺服器。

When you choose to download a file, it is saved to a location you select via the system file picker. Downloads are user-initiated and are never uploaded to any server.

6. 兒童隱私Children's privacy

本應用不針對兒童設計，亦不收集兒童的任何資料。

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. 政策變更Changes to this policy

本政策如有更新，將於本頁公布並更新上方的生效日期。

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date above.
